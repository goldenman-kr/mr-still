Mr. Still — Privacy Policy

2026-10-24

Mr. Still is a local image viewer. It does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, track users, or send images, metadata, file names, folder paths, or usage information to the developer or a server.

The app reads the images and folders you open. Image metadata, including embedded GPS coordinates if present, is displayed locally. The app does not request your device's current location. The App Store edition accesses files and folders you authorize through macOS and stores security-scoped bookmarks on your Mac to restore access after relaunch.

Preferences, window layout, favorites, recent items, and file-access bookmarks are saved locally. Removing an item from favorites or recent items removes that list entry; it does not delete the original image or folder. To remove the app's remaining local settings and bookmarks, remove its local application data after quitting the app. Copying, pasting, deleting, revealing files in Finder, and printing occur only when you request those actions. Printing and files stored in cloud-synced folders follow the behavior and privacy policies of your selected macOS services, printer, and storage provider.

The app does not sell or share personal data. If you choose to contact the developer through GitHub, GitHub processes information you provide under its own privacy policy. Do not include private photos, paths, or metadata in public issue reports. Apple handles App Store purchases and any optional system diagnostics under Apple's policies; Mr. Still has no separate payment or analytics service.
